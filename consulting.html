Management consulting & fractional CFO

Operations and financial discipline for small and mid-sized businesses, and practical help for small businesses entering government contracting.

The practice draws on our principal's five years as a small-business growth and management consultant and fractional CFO, advising nine small businesses with $1.5M–$3M in EBITDA on valuation enhancement and strategic planning, and seven years running Amazon last-mile and sortation operations (600+ associates, 30+ 3PL partners). UCLA Anderson MBA.

Operations and supply chain

  • Operations diagnostic. Where time, cost and errors are going, measured against the numbers you already have.
  • SOP, KPI and scorecard build-out. Written procedures, a small set of metrics that people are held to, and the review rhythm that keeps them in use.
  • Network and cost-to-serve analysis. Which lanes, customers and service levels make money, and which don't.
  • Carrier and 3PL performance management. Scorecards, and rate and accessorial invoice audit.

Financial operations and fractional CFO

  • Pricing and margin. A cost model that tells you what each job has to sell for.
  • Cash and budget governance. A rolling cash forecast, a budget with named owners, and a monthly close.
  • Valuation and strategic planning. What drives the value of the business and a plan to move it.
  • Books for government work. Direct and indirect costs separated and costs collected by contract, structured around the SF 1408 pre-award accounting-system checklist.

Entering government contracting

  • Readiness review. Which registrations and certifications you qualify for, in what order, and what each one is worth in your line of work.
  • Opportunity screening. Reading a solicitation for the requirements that decide whether to bid: eligibility, evaluation method, the compliance items, and the real deadline.
  • Bid support. Compliance matrix, bid calendar, cost build-up and the assembled proposal package, with you as the offeror.
  • After award. Reporting, insurance and flow-down tracking, and invoice packages in the agency's format.

What this is not. We don't sell contracts, leads or guaranteed awards, and we are not affiliated with the SBA, GSA or any agency. SAM.gov registration and SBA and California certifications are free to apply for, and you submit them yourself; we show you how. We don't provide legal, tax or audit services.
