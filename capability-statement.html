About Us

Bastion Supply & Logistics is a service-disabled veteran-owned California small business (SBA-certified SDVOSB / VOSB) at the Los Angeles / Long Beach port market, founded in 2020. We are an asset-light third-party logistics (3PL) and supply-chain consulting firm — we coordinate freight, warehousing, and distribution as your 3PL, and improve the operation behind it with Amazon-scale consulting, fractional-CFO discipline, and an in-house AI capture engine. Specialty/regulated freight, fuel, and facilities are delivered through the same partner network. Bid-ready as prime or subcontractor.

Core Competencies

Third-Party Logistics (3PL) (core)
  • Freight arrangement & carrier management; dispatch, tendering & track-and-trace across a vetted SoCal network
  • Warehousing, cross-dock & port-adjacent transload coordination
  • Specialty & regulated freight — drayage, bulk fuel, hazmat, refrigerated — through carrier partners
Supply-Chain Consulting & Capture (core)
  • Network & route optimization, SOP build-out, KPI & scorecard design, operations diagnostics — the Amazon playbook at SMB scale
  • AI-driven capture & proposal support — multi-portal opportunity scan, compliance / RTM matrix, cost build-up
  • Fractional-CFO support: pricing, margin, cash & budget governance
Facilities Support
  • Facilities support — janitorial, grounds & building-exterior care, pressure washing, lot sweeping; truck & fleet washing
