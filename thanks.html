Got it. Here's exactly what happens next.

Your rate request is in. No auto-responder loop, no "an agent will be with you shortly" — the sequence below is what we actually do.

  1. We read it today — a person, not a queue. If anything in the lane is ambiguous (terminal, chassis, appointment, weight), we ask you one short question rather than guessing and quoting wrong.
  2. We go to carriers who actually run your lane. We check operating authority against FMCSA and hold a cargo certificate issued directly by the carrier's agent before anything is tendered. A carrier we can't verify doesn't get your freight.
  3. Same business day, you get a rate with the carrier named. Linehaul plus any accessorial we can foresee, itemized. If we can't cover the lane at a price that works for you, we say so that day instead of quoting to stall.
New shipper? The first three loads are prepaid by ACH or card before pickup; standard terms after that, subject to credit approval. We say it here rather than at the invoice.
Clock already running? If you have a last free day inside 48 hours, don't wait on email — call (213) 788-9999 and say "LFD". Per-diem is cheaper to prevent than to dispute.
