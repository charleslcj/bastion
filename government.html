For primes and teaming partners

For primes and partners we run the bid side: qualify the opportunity, break down the solicitation, check compliance, size up the competition, plan delivery, build and submit the proposal, and handle post-award reporting and paperwork.

  • On an SDVOSB set-aside. Because Bastion is itself SBA-certified SDVOSB, work you subcontract to us is "similarly situated" under 13 CFR 125.6 — it does not count against the 50% you must self-perform on an SDVOSB set-aside.
  • On California contracts. We hold California DVBE certification #2052517. We keep the DVBE commercially-useful-function file and prepare the prime's STD 817 (or Caltrans CEM-2402S) so nothing is held back at closeout.
  • After award. We run the paperwork that keeps the contract paid: subcontractor utilization and participation reporting, certificates of insurance and flow-down tracking, certified-payroll collection where prevailing wage applies, and invoice packages in the agency's format.
  • Tooling. We've built an internal, human-in-the-loop AI capability that finds the government opportunities actually worth bidding, builds the operations plan behind them, and assembles the proposal packet.

Every teaming arrangement states in writing which part of the work Bastion performs. We don't lend a certification to work we aren't doing.
